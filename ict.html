ICT & Packaging Training

Learn the essential skills for digital technology and product packaging. These courses are designed for applicants who want practical knowledge and career-ready preparation.

Information and Communication Technology (ICT)

ICT introduces students to computer systems, software tools, and online communication. You will learn about word processing, spreadsheets, digital presentations, and internet safety. This course helps applicants build strong computer skills for school, work, and everyday life.

Packaging Fundamentals

Packaging teaches materials, product presentation, and safe handling. Students learn how to choose the right packaging, wrap products professionally, and protect items during transport. This course is perfect for those interested in retail, logistics, and customer service roles.

Why Apply?

  • Gain practical skills in technology and workplace packaging.
  • Improve your confidence in computer use and product preparation.
  • Prepare for entry-level jobs in offices, retail, and service industries.
