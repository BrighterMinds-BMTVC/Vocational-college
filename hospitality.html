Hospitality Course

Build essential skills for working in hotels, restaurants, and customer service roles with confidence, professionalism, and practical training.

Course Overview

This hospitality course teaches the fundamentals of guest service, workplace cleanliness, communication, and safe food handling. It is designed for learners preparing for entry-level positions in hotels, events, restaurants, and customer-facing service environments.

What You Will Learn

  • How to welcome guests and provide friendly, professional service.
  • Basic front desk and reception duties.
  • Table service, dining room support, and event assistance.
  • Food safety, hygiene, and workplace cleanliness standards.
  • Effective teamwork, communication, and problem solving.

Course Highlights

The course combines practical skill-building with real-world hospitality examples so students can confidently step into service roles and deliver a great guest experience.

  • Customer service best practices
  • Managing guest requests and enquiries
  • Setting tables and serving food and drinks
  • Maintaining a safe and clean work area
  • Understanding hospitality career pathways

Who Should Apply?

This course is ideal for students who enjoy working with people, want to develop service skills, and are interested in starting a career in hospitality, tourism, or customer service.
