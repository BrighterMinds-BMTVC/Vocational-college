Care Giving Course

Learn the essential skills for supporting people with health or daily living needs in a safe, caring, and respectful way.

Course Introduction

This course prepares students for caregiving roles in home care, community support, and healthcare settings. It focuses on practical skills, empathy, communication, and safe care practices.

What the Course Covers

  • Understanding the responsibilities of a caregiver
  • Helping with personal care and daily living activities
  • Creating safe and comfortable environments
  • Supporting people with mobility, hygiene, and nutrition
  • Communicating respectfully with clients and families

Learning Steps

  1. Start by learning the role of a caregiver and key qualities needed in the job.
  2. Study how to assist with daily tasks such as bathing, dressing, and eating.
  3. Practice safe lifting, moving, and helping someone around the home.
  4. Learn how to recognize basic health needs and when to ask for professional help.
  5. Develop strong communication and respect for each person’s privacy and choices.

Why This Course Is Useful

Students will gain confidence working with people who need extra care, and they will learn skills that are valuable for jobs in social care, nursing homes, and support services.

  • Build caring and reliable work habits
  • Learn how to help others stay healthy and comfortable
  • Prepare for practical, entry-level caregiving roles
  • Understand safety, hygiene, and emotional support
