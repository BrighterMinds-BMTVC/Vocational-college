Barista Making Course

Learn how to prepare delicious coffee drinks, serve customers professionally, and build confidence in a busy café environment.

What This Course Covers

This course introduces students to the basics of coffee preparation, drink presentation, and food service standards. It is perfect for learners who want to work in cafés, restaurants, hotels, or start a career in hospitality.

Key Topics

Students will learn about coffee beans, brewing methods, milk steaming, espresso making, latte art, customer service, hygiene, and workplace safety.

Why Join the Course?

  • Learn practical barista skills used in real café settings.
  • Improve your confidence in preparing hot and cold beverages.
  • Develop customer service and teamwork skills for hospitality jobs.
  • Gain knowledge that can lead to employment in the food and beverage industry.

Course Highlights

  • Introduction to coffee types and flavor profiles
  • Espresso and cappuccino preparation
  • Milk frothing and drink presentation
  • Cleanliness, hygiene, and safe food handling
  • Customer interaction and service etiquette
