Beauty Course

Discover the skills needed for makeup, skincare, nail care, and salon service in a friendly and practical beauty training course.

Course Introduction

This course teaches new students the essential beauty techniques used in salons and spas. It is perfect for beginners who want to learn how to care for skin, create polished looks, and work confidently with clients.

What You Will Learn

  • Basic skincare routines and hygiene for healthy skin.
  • Makeup application for day and evening looks.
  • Nail care and simple manicure techniques.
  • Customer service and professional salon behavior.
  • How to create a clean, welcoming beauty space.

Course Structure

  1. Begin with skin health, cleansing, moisturizing, and safety.
  2. Learn colour theory, tools, and makeup application steps.
  3. Practice basic nail care, filing, polishing, and presentation.
  4. Develop communication skills for talking with clients and understanding their needs.
  5. Complete a simple beauty service routine from start to finish.

Why Take This Course?

By the end of the course, students will feel prepared to begin entry-level work in beauty salons, makeup studios, or as a personal beauty assistant.

  • Gain hands-on beauty skills for real-world settings.
  • Build confidence in appearance and client care.
  • Learn how to work safely and professionally.
  • Explore opportunities in beauty and wellness services.
